It combines satellite-based environmental monitoring, wildlife habitat analysis, disaster risk assessment and satellite communication scheduling to help forest-monitoring teams understand environmental threats, identify vulnerable ecosystems, predict satellite contact opportunities and plan field-report delivery, even under simulated satellite-service disruptions.

Operational Context

Bridging Orbital Earth Observation with Ranger Ground Reality

India's forest rangers protect over 713,000 square kilometres of biodiversity. Traditional monitoring workflows are disconnected from high-tempo satellite telemetry.

CHALLENGE 01 The Ground Blindspot

What Forest Teams Struggle With

Field rangers face fragmented spatial data, delayed thermal fire alerts from raw satellite passes, and severe communication blackouts during monsoon or cyclonic weather. When field incidents occur in deep gorges or estuarine creeks, reports sit un-transmitted because patrol squads don't know when the next low-Earth orbit satellite window opens.

Field Patrol Squads Range Beat Officers Watchtower Crews
SOLUTION 02 The HABITAT IQ Engine

What HABITAT IQ Does

HABITAT IQ fuses multi-spectral vegetation indices (VEDAS), weather radar (MOSDAC), and high-resolution radar/optical passes (Bhoonidhi & Bhuvan) into a unified risk deck. It calculates 7-day predictive risk curves, maps wildlife corridor bottlenecks, and predicts upcoming satellite pass windows—scheduling field dispatch automatically even when links fail.

APPLICATION 03 Field Deployment

Who It Is For

Designed for Divisional Forest Officers directing anti-poaching and fire crews, Wildlife Researchers safeguarding vulnerable tiger and rhino corridors across fractured habitats, and Disaster Response Units (NDRF / SDRF) mobilizing emergency flood or landslide evacuations.

Architecture & Data Pipeline

How HABITAT IQ Operates

A four-tier operational lifecycle transforming raw orbital remote sensing into dependable ranger actions.

01

Multi-Mission Telemetry

Ingests multispectral, synthetic aperture radar (SAR), and thermal data from ISRO portals (Bhuvan, MOSDAC, VEDAS, and Bhoonidhi) over scheduled orbital passes.

Sensor Ingest Layer
02

Machine Analytics & Anomaly Detection

Algorithms compute vegetation health (NDVI), canopy cover changes, soil saturation thresholds, and thermal heat signatures to detect threats before they escalate.

Spatial Intelligence Engine
03

Disaster Risk & Corridor Modeling

Translates complex remote-sensing indicators into human-readable 0-100 risk scores, 7-day vulnerability forecasts, and animal corridor pinch-point advisories.

Tactical Decision Layer
04

Resilient Report Delivery

Calculates exact satellite pass windows for field teams. When space weather or hardware outages occur, the planner shifts seamlessly to delay-tolerant store-and-forward routing.

Authoritative Data Foundations

ISRO Earth Observation Portals

HABITAT IQ adheres strictly to data streams powered by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

Bhuvan

NRSC · ISRO (National Remote Sensing Centre)
bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in ↗

ISRO's flagship geospatial portal providing high-resolution multi-spectral Indian Earth Observation imagery, base cadastre boundaries, and near-real-time active forest fire hotspot alerts from the Forest Survey of India (FSI) sensor feeds.

Primary Payloads: Cartosat-2/3 PAN, LISS-IV, Thematic Forest Cover
Cadence: Near Real-Time (6-12 hr latency)

MOSDAC

SAC · ISRO (Space Applications Centre)
mosdac.gov.in ↗

Meteorological & Oceanographic Satellite Data Archival Centre delivering geostationary thermal infrared imagery, cloud microphysics, rainfall estimates, and oceanic wave swell tracking from INSAT and Oceansat satellites.

Primary Payloads: INSAT-3D / 3DR Imager & Sounder, Oceansat-3
Cadence: Continuous Half-Hourly Broadcasts

VEDAS

SAC · ISRO (Space Applications Centre)
vedas.sac.gov.in ↗

Visualisation of Earth observation Data and Archival System specialized in vegetation index (NDVI/EVI) time series, land degradation, desertification, forest biomass dynamics, and slope stability modeling for hilly terrain.

Primary Payloads: Resourcesat-2A AWiFS, RISAT-1A SAR Biomass
Cadence: 8-Day and 15-Day Composite Baselines

Bhoonidhi

NRSC · ISRO (National Remote Sensing Centre)
bhoonidhi.nrsc.gov.in ↗

National Remote Sensing Centre open data dissemination gateway providing calibrated synthetic aperture radar (SAR) backscatter from EOS-04, cartographic digital elevation models (DEM), and multi-spectral LISS sensor suites.

Primary Payloads: EOS-04 (RISAT-1A C-band SAR), Resourcesat-2/2A
Cadence: Daily Low-Earth Orbit Pass Ingest
Transparent Data Policy

Where real-time programmatic API connectivity is restricted or behind government intranet gateways, HABITAT IQ renders high-fidelity simulated datasets modeled accurately on ISRO orbital parameters, sensor resolutions, and RFC 5050 DTN protocols.

Design Philosophy: Utility Over Cliché

Most modern tech demos rely on neon purple gradients, floating cards with glowing drop shadows, and filler corporate buzzwords. HABITAT IQ was crafted from the ground up to mirror the dignity, restraint, and operational clarity of an authentic field-research organization. Every metric has a unit, every risk score has a physical explanation, and every satellite window is grounded in real orbital mechanics.
